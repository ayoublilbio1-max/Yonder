# Testing Yonder — a guide to every feature

> ⏱ **In a hurry?** Do the **Quick test** just below. It takes about 10 minutes and shows the main features.
> **Have more time?** Skip it and do the **Full test** after it. It covers every feature, step by step.

Yonder is a **demo** dating app (Android). There is no server: your account and everything you do
stay on this phone. The other people are demo profiles, and their likes, matches and messages are
**simulated** with the fixed rules below, so you can try every feature in a few minutes.

Nothing is ever charged. Every payment screen is a demo.

> Tip: when you've tried everything, go to **Profile › ⚙ Settings › Restart the demo** to start
> again with a fresh deck (your account and profile stay).

---

## Quick test (about 10 minutes)

1. Sign up, add 3 photos and check your phone with the demo code.
2. Like the first person: **match**. Say hi and wait for the reply.
3. Pass 4 people, then like the next one: **match**.
4. Super like 1 person: **match** (first super like). Then super like 3 more people: the 3rd one is a **match**.
5. Send 3 messages in a chat: you get a **timed photo**. Open it once.
6. Match with a locked profile and tap **Request photos**: the photos are shared.
7. Buy **Gold** and open **Likes**: like someone back for an **instant match**.
8. Buy a **Boost** and watch the countdown.
9. Switch the language to **Français**, then back.
10. Turn on the **Passcode** and reopen the app.
11. **Restart the demo** and do it again.

The rules behind these steps are explained in the Full test below.

## Full test

Read the simulated rules first, then follow sections 1 to 8.

### The simulated rules (read this first)

#### Matches

| What you do                             | Result                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your **first like**                     | Always a match                                                                                          |
| Your **first super like**               | Always a match                                                                                          |
| After that, a **like**                  | A match once you've swiped **5 people** since your last match (likes, passes and super likes all count) |
| After that, **super likes**             | Every **3rd super like** is a match                                                                     |
| Like someone from **Likes › Likes you** | **Instant match** (they already liked you)                                                              |

_Example:_ match → pass, pass, like, pass → the next like (the 5th swipe) is a match.

#### People who like you

- At the start, **2 people** like you.
- **1 more person** likes you every **4 swipes** (up to 30). You get a "Someone new likes you" notification.
- Without Gold, their photos are blurred. With **Yonder Gold** you see who they are.

#### Chats

- About **half of your matches write first**, a few seconds after the match.
- They **"type"** (three dots) and **reply to every message** you send, after 2–3 seconds.
- Sometimes (about 1 in 3) they **❤️ your message**.
- **Timed photo:** each match sends you **one timed photo with their 3rd reply**. You can open it **once**.
- **Your timed photo:** they open it, then reply.

#### Private (locked) photos

Some profiles hide their photos ("hasn't shared her photos yet"). They unlock:

- after you send them **3 messages**, or
- about **4 seconds** after you tap **Request photos** in the chat.

Once shared, they stay shared.

#### They can unmatch you

Like real apps, at most **one match per app start** may unmatch you. It's only a match you
**never wrote to**, matched **more than 30 minutes ago** (about 1 in 3 of them).
The chat disappears and you get a notification.

#### The deck

- People you swipe leave the deck **until you close and reopen the app**. Matches and blocked or
  reported people **never come back**.
- **Rewind** brings back your last swipe (not a match).
- When you've seen everyone, you can **increase the distance**, **widen the age range** or
  **rewind the profiles you passed**.

### 1. Sign up and onboarding

1. Open Yonder. On the welcome screen, tap the **🌐 globe** to change the language
   (English, Français, Español, Nederlands).
2. Sign up with one of:
   - **Google** (real Google sign-in, or a demo account chooser if it isn't available)
   - **Apple** (simulated: name, then Share / Hide my email)
   - **Email** (a real account on this phone: the password needs 8+ characters, a letter and a number)
3. Answer the onboarding steps: gender, first name, birthday, what you're looking for, marital
   status, children, height, smoking, drinking, religion, profession, education, nationality,
   interests, personality, bio and photos.
4. Add **3 or more photos** from the gallery or the camera.
5. **Phone check (simulated):** type any number. A demo message banner "receives" the 6-digit
   code a moment later. Type it in. No SMS is sent.
6. You land on **Swipe**, and Android asks to allow notifications. Allow them to test the
   notification features. You get a **"Welcome to Yonder 🎉"** notification.

### 2. Swipe

1. **Swipe right** = like, **left** = pass, **up** = super like. You can also use the buttons:
   Rewind · Pass · Super like · Like · Send compliment.
2. Get your first **match** with your first like. You'll see the match screen and hear the match sound.
3. Follow the match rules above to get more matches.
4. **Tap the photo** to open the full-screen viewer (swipe between photos and **videos with sound**).
   Some profiles have videos.
5. Tap a **locked profile's** photo: "Her photos unlock after you match".
6. Scroll down a card to see the whole profile, including **Your similarities** (what you have in common).
7. At the bottom of a profile: **Share**, **Favorite**, **Block**, **Report**.
8. **Rewind** your last swipe.
9. **Top bar:**
   - **Filters**: age and distance sliders plus more options (some are Gold only). If too few people
     match, you get a note and similar people are shown.
   - **Sort**: Recommended, age, distance, just joined, recently active, verified, complete bio,
     and "I'm in all their filters".
   - **🔔 Notifications**: recent activity. New items have a dot.
   - **⚡ Boost**: buy boosts (1 / 5 / 10). A boost lasts 3 hours, with a countdown in the top bar.
10. **Send compliment:** 1 free per day, then buy packs (3 / 10 / 25).

### 3. Explore

1. Open **Explore**. Categories: Ready for marriage, Something serious, New friends, Just for fun,
   Verified, Just joined, With a video, Travel lovers, Foodies, Fitness, Music & concerts,
   Outdoors, Book lovers, Gamers, Pet lovers.
2. Each one opens its own swipe deck, with the same match rules.
3. **Active now** and **Nearby** are **Gold only**.

### 4. Likes

1. **Likes you** shows the people who liked you (blurred without Gold).
2. Tap **Yonder Gold** and pay in the demo checkout (card or Google Pay, nothing charged). You
   hear the purchase sound, and the photos are no longer blurred.
3. Open someone and **Like** them back: **instant match**.
4. **Favorites** lists the people you starred.
5. The **number badge** on the Likes tab counts new likes and clears when you open the tab.

### 5. Chats

1. The top row shows **New matches**. Below them are your conversations, with **All / Unread / Read** filters.
2. The **number badge** on the Chats tab counts unread messages.
3. Open a chat and send a message: they type, then reply. Send 3 messages to get their **timed photo**.
4. **Long-press** one of their messages to ❤️ it (long-press again removes it).
5. Send real media:
   - **Gallery**: a photo or a video (up to 60 s)
   - **Camera**: take a photo. You can set a **timer**: normal, view once, 5 s or 10 s.
   - **Mic**: record a voice message, then play it back
6. **Locked profile:** tap **Request photos**. They share them about 4 seconds later.
7. Tap **Chat | Profile** at the top to see their whole profile.
8. **Audio / video call** (simulated): "Calling…" → "Ringing…" → no answer after 15 s. A video
   call shows your front camera.
9. **⋮ Chat options:** Share profile, Search chat, Export chat (a text file you can share),
   Unmatch, Block, Report and block.
10. Leave a chat and wait for a reply. You get a **notification**, and tapping it opens the chat.

### 6. Profile

1. The ring around your photo shows how **complete** your profile is, with a hint for what's missing.
2. **Preview**: see your profile the way others see it.
3. **Edit**: change photos (add, make main, remove) and every answer.
4. **Verify your photo** (simulated): copy the pose in a selfie, wait for "Checking…", then you
   get a **blue check**. The selfie is never saved.
5. See your plan (Basic / Gold), your compliments and your boosts.

### 7. Settings (Profile › ⚙)

| Setting                      | What to try                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Notifications**            | Turn all off, or choose types: matches, messages, likes, boosts, daily picks (7 pm), welcome. Chat message preview on/off.   |
| **Appearance**               | Follow phone, Light, Dark                                                                                                    |
| **Distance**                 | Km or Mi                                                                                                                     |
| **Language**                 | Follow phone, English, Français, Español, Nederlands. The app redraws in the new language.                                   |
| **App sounds**               | Match, boost and payment sounds on or off                                                                                    |
| **Manage purchases**         | The list of your demo purchases                                                                                              |
| **Passcode**                 | Set a 4-digit code. Close Yonder, or leave it for more than 1 minute: it asks for the code. "Forgot passcode?" logs you out. |
| **Who can see my profile**   | Everyone · Only people I like · No one. With "No one" you get no matches and no new likes.                                   |
| **Profile sharing link**     | Turn it on, then share your profile link                                                                                     |
| **Profiles I've blocked**    | Unblock people                                                                                                               |
| **Block my phone contacts**  | Demo contacts (never your real ones)                                                                                         |
| **Feedback**                 | Send a bug report or an idea                                                                                                 |
| **Log out**                  | Your account stays on this phone. Sign in again to continue.                                                                 |
| **Restart the demo**         | Clears swipes, matches, chats, Gold and purchases. Keeps your account and profile.                                           |
| **Delete my Yonder profile** | Deletes everything from this phone, then signs you out                                                                       |

### 8. Phone notifications

You get these even when Yonder is closed or in the background:

- **Welcome to Yonder** (the first time), then **Welcome back** each time you open the app
  (or come back after 5+ minutes)
- **It's a match!**
- A **new message** (when you're not in that chat)
- **Someone new likes you** / **Someone super liked you**
- **Your boost is on** and, 3 hours later, **Your boost has ended**
- **Daily picks** at 7 pm
- **They unmatched you**

Tap a notification to open the right screen.
