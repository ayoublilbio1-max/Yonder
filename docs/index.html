Yonder

A demo dating app for Android: swipe, match and chat.

What Yonder is

Yonder is a portfolio demo that shows how a modern dating app works: profiles with photos and videos, swiping, matches, likes and chat. It is not a public dating service.

Your data stays on your phone

Everything you do in the demo is saved only on your device. Yonder has no server that stores your account, messages or photos.
