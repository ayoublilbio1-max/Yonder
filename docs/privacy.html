Yonder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Yonder is a demo dating app made to show how a dating app works. This page explains what the app uses, where it is kept, and your choices.

1. Information the app uses

  • Google sign-in (optional): if you choose "Continue with Google", the app receives your name, email address and profile photo from Google. It uses them only to show your name and photo inside the app.
  • Apple and email sign-in: these are simulated in the demo. An email and password you type are saved only on your phone.
  • Profile details you enter: such as your name, birthday, photos, bio and preferences. They are saved only on your phone.
  • Photos and camera: used only when you choose to add profile photos or take a selfie. Photos stay on your phone.
  • Microphone: used only when you record a voice message in a chat. Recordings stay on your phone.
  • Location (optional): used while the app is open to show demo profiles near you. It is not sent to any server.
  • Notifications (optional): used for local reminders and match alerts created on your phone.

2. Where your information is kept

Yonder has no account server and no database. Everything you enter or create (your account, profile, likes, matches and messages) is saved only on your device, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. What is shared

Yonder does not sell, share or send your personal information to anyone. The other profiles in the app are demo content. Their photos and videos load from a file host (Cloudflare R2), which, like any website, can see your device's IP address when media loads.

4. Google user data

Yonder's use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to show your name and photo in the app, is never transferred to others, and is never used for advertising.

5. Your choices

  • You can use the demo without Google sign-in.
  • You can refuse or turn off camera, microphone, location and notification permissions in your phone's settings.
  • You can delete all your data by uninstalling the app, or with Settings › Apps › Yonder › Storage › Clear data.
  • You can remove Yonder's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Children

Yonder is a dating demo and is not meant for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, use the in-app Feedback option (Settings › Help › Feedback).
